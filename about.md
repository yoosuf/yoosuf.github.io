Hi, I'm Yoosuf Mohamed, a Systems Architect based in Colombo, Sri Lanka, with over a decade of experience helping startups and enterprises build AI automation and AI-first products that scale. I specialise in architecting systems that bridge the gap between cutting-edge AI and production-grade software.

My expertise spans the full AI stack — from designing LLM-powered applications and RAG pipelines to building scalable backend systems in Go, Elixir, and FastAPI. On the frontend, I work across Node, NestJS, NextJS, ReactJS, and Flutter to deliver seamless, cross-platform experiences.

I'm passionate about AI automation — building intelligent systems that reduce toil, amplify human capability, and create measurable business value. Whether it's a lean startup moving fast or an enterprise modernising legacy workflows, I focus on solutions that are pragmatic, maintainable, and built to scale.

Beyond AI, I bring deep experience across the systems architecture lifecycle: cloud infrastructure (AWS), search (ElasticSearch), caching (Redis), API design (GraphQL, REST), event-driven architectures, and microservices. I believe in clean code, solid architecture, and shipping products that make a real difference.

I'm always learning — right now that means going deeper on LLM orchestration, agentic workflows, and the Elixir/OTP ecosystem. If you're building something ambitious, I'd love to hear about it.
