## How pricing works

All pricing is quoted and invoiced exclusively in **USD** (United States Dollar). Engagement fees are determined by:

- **Scope** — the size and complexity of the work
- **Timeline** — how quickly the work needs to be delivered
- **Engagement model** — project, retainer, or fractional arrangement

Smaller, well-defined engagements have the starting prices shown on the [Services page](/services/). Larger or more complex projects are individually scoped and quoted separately. You always receive a written proposal before any work begins — there are no surprise charges.

## The quoting process

1. **Free discovery call** — a 30-minute call to understand your goals, constraints, and what success looks like.
2. **Written proposal** — a fixed-price proposal covering deliverables, timeline, milestones, and payment schedule.
3. **Agreement** — the proposal is formalised in an engagement agreement, and work begins only after both parties have signed.

## Payment terms

Unless otherwise stated in the engagement agreement, payments work as follows:

- **Deposit** — a deposit is required to reserve capacity before work begins.
- **Milestones** — remaining fees are invoiced against agreed milestones as defined in the engagement agreement.
- **Retainers** — monthly retainers are invoiced in advance and paid at the start of each billing period.

Work on the project does not proceed while an invoice is outstanding.

## Invoicing

- Invoices are issued in USD and reference the milestone or deliverable they relate to.
- Invoices are sent by email and are payable within the terms stated on the invoice (typically 7 days).
- All invoices are accompanied by a breakdown of work completed, so you always know what you are paying for.

## Payment methods

Payments are accepted via [Wise.com](https://wise.com/) (recommended for fast, low-cost international transfers) or by direct bank transfer. Bank and Wise account details are provided on the invoice. Receipts are provided for every payment.

## Taxes

Prices are quoted exclusive of any applicable taxes, levies, or duties. If you are required to pay any tax on the services (including VAT, GST, or withholding tax), you are responsible for paying it. Where the Consultant is legally required to charge a tax, it will be added to the invoice at the applicable rate.

## Engagement agreements

Every engagement is governed by a signed agreement that defines deliverables, milestones, and payment terms, safeguarding the interests of both parties. The agreement is the authoritative document for the engagement — these pages summarise the general terms, but the signed agreement takes precedence.

## Revisions and rework

Each milestone includes reasonable revisions to bring the deliverable in line with the agreed scope. Substantial changes to scope are treated as new work and quoted separately. Rework caused by changes requested by the client, rather than defects in the deliverable, is billed at the standard rate.

## Refunds

Deposits and completed milestone payments are non-refundable once the corresponding work has been delivered. If the Consultant fails to deliver agreed work, you are entitled to a refund for the undelivered portion, and the engagement agreement describes how this is handled.

## Late payment

Work is paused on any project with an outstanding invoice. Repeatedly late payments may result in a pause of the engagement until payments are brought current, as described in the engagement agreement.

## Termination

Either party may terminate an engagement with written notice as specified in the engagement agreement. Upon termination, the client is responsible for payment for all work completed up to the termination date, including any work completed but not yet delivered.

## Intellectual property

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, deliverable work products created specifically for you during a paid engagement are owned by you upon full payment. Pre-existing tools, libraries, methods, and intellectual property of the Consultant remain the property of the Consultant.
