## 1. Currency and Quotes

All pricing is quoted and invoiced exclusively in **USD** (United States Dollar). Engagement fees are determined by the following factors, each of which shall be assessed by the Consultant in its sole discretion:

- **Scope** — the size, complexity, and nature of the work;
- **Timeline** — the delivery schedule and any priority or expedited delivery requirements;

The starting prices published on the [Services page](/services/) are indicative only and do not constitute a binding offer. Larger or more complex engagements are individually scoped and quoted separately, and no work shall commence until the Client has received and accepted a written proposal setting out the fixed or estimated Fees.

## 2. Quoting Process

The engagement process is as follows:

1. **Discovery call.** A complimentary thirty (30) minute call to understand the Client's goals, constraints, and definition of success.
2. **Written proposal.** The Consultant issues a written proposal specifying the deliverables, timeline, milestones, and Fees. The proposal may include assumptions and exclusions, which shall be binding on the parties.
3. **Engagement Agreement.** Upon acceptance of the proposal, the parties execute an Engagement Agreement, and work begins only after the Engagement Agreement has been executed by both parties and any required deposit has been received.

## 3. Payment Terms

Unless otherwise stated in the Engagement Agreement, payments are due as follows:

- **Deposit.** A deposit is required to reserve capacity before work begins. The deposit amount shall be specified in the Engagement Agreement.
- **Milestones.** Remaining Fees are invoiced against agreed milestones as defined in the Engagement Agreement. Milestone invoices are due upon delivery of the corresponding milestone.
- **Retainers.** Monthly retainers are invoiced in advance and are due at the start of each billing period.

The Consultant shall not be obligated to commence or continue work while any invoice is outstanding and unpaid. Suspension of Services for non-payment shall not constitute a breach by the Consultant.

## 4. Invoicing

- Invoices are issued in USD and reference the milestone or deliverable to which they relate.
- Invoices are sent by email and are payable within the period stated on the invoice, typically seven (7) days from the invoice date.
- Each invoice is accompanied by a breakdown of the work completed, so that the Client can verify the amounts billed.

The Consultant does not accept payment by credit or debit card unless otherwise agreed in writing in the Engagement Agreement.
