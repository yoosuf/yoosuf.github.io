## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how yoosuf.me (the "Site"), owned and operated by Yoosuf Mohamed, collects, uses, and protects information. The Site is a personal blog and portfolio that describes consulting services; it does not require user accounts and does not host user-generated content.

## 2. Information We Collect

The Site collects limited information:

- **Analytics data.** The Site uses Google Analytics to understand aggregate visitor behaviour, including pages visited, approximate location, device type, and referral source. This data is anonymised and does not identify individual visitors.
- **Server logs.** Hosting infrastructure may record standard server logs such as IP address, browser type, and timestamps.
- **Information you choose to share.** If you contact the Consultant by email, by booking a call, or by any other channel, the information you provide is used solely to respond to your enquiry.

## 3. Cookies

The Site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to measure traffic and usage patterns. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, or use browser extensions that block tracking scripts. Disabling cookies does not affect your ability to read the Site.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The Site relies on the following third-party services:

- **Google Analytics** — usage analytics. Google's privacy policy applies to data it processes.
- **Google Fonts** — web fonts. Google may log requests to its font servers.
- **Cal.com** — booking. If you book a call through the Site, Cal.com's privacy policy governs that interaction.

These services are outside the control of the Consultant and have their own privacy policies.

## 5. How Information Is Used

Collected information is used to:

- Understand how the Site is used and improve its content and performance.
- Respond to enquiries and provide consulting services.
- Comply with legal obligations.

Information is never sold or rented to third parties.

## 6. Data Retention

Analytics data is retained according to Google Analytics' retention settings. Communications with the Consultant are retained for as long as necessary to manage enquiries and engagements.

## 7. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information the Consultant holds about you by emailing [mayoosuf@gmail.com](mailto:mayoosuf@gmail.com). The Consultant will respond within a reasonable timeframe.

## 8. Security

Reasonable technical and organisational measures are taken to protect information. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
